QUIC Deployment in ASEAN and Vietnam

Measurements and visualizations of QUIC protocol adoption across ASEAN, with a focused longitudinal study of Vietnam's Internet infrastructure.

ASEAN Map Vietnam Map

QUIC [RFC 9000] is a network transport protocol originally developed by Google and standardized by the IETF in 2021. It improves performance, reduces latency, and strengthens security for Internet connections. As Vietnam's digital economy continues its rapid growth [e-Conomy SEA 2024], understanding the adoption of modern protocols like QUIC is important for optimizing network infrastructure.

About This Project

Quick UDP Internet Connections (QUIC), an advanced IETF transport-layer protocol, offers significant advantages over traditional TCP, including reduced latency, enhanced multiplexing, built-in encryption, and optimized handshake processes. Because of these benefits, QUIC has been widely deployed by major Internet companies, and previous research has observed increasing global adoption.

Motivated by this trend, we investigate QUIC deployment in Vietnam using Vietnamese IPv4 datasets. Our scanning results indicate that QUIC adoption in Vietnam remains in the early stages, with a low deployment rate of approximately 0.05% of all Vietnamese IPv4 addresses. We also analyze active migration support, TLS encryption preferences, geographic deployment patterns, and performance differences between QUIC and TCP across Wi-Fi and mobile networks.
